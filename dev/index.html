HighDimensionalModels.jl

HighDimensionalModels fits the Gaussian lasso at a single penalty level, lasso (with the elastic net through the keyword alpha), and the square-root lasso, sqrt_lasso. The Gaussian lasso solves

\[\min_{\beta \in \mathbb{R}^p}\ \frac{1}{2n}\lVert y - X\beta\rVert_2^2 + \lambda \lVert \beta \rVert_1 .\]

It is a thin layer. ProximalBase.jl owns the mathematics (the loss, the penalty and their numerical contracts) and ProximalSolvers.jl owns the solver (coordinate descent and its stopping rule). Both check their arguments. This package forms the problem, calls the solver and returns the coefficients, the residuals and the solver's result.

using HighDimensionalModels, Random

rng = Xoshiro(1)
n, p = 100, 20
X = randn(rng, n, p)
y = X[:, 1:3] * [2.0, -1.5, 1.0] + 0.5 * randn(rng, n)

fit = lasso(X, y, 0.1, CDSolver(order = :cyclic, momentum = false))
println(count(!iszero, coef(fit)), " nonzero coefficients of ", p)
println(round.(coef(fit)[1:5]; digits = 2))
5 nonzero coefficients of 20
[1.9, -1.35, 0.94, 0.0, 0.0]

The keyword alpha, with 0 < alpha ≤ 1, gives the elastic net. The lasso page gives the inputs, the result, how convergence is reported and how to check a fit independently. The square-root lasso page gives the normalized objective of sqrt_lasso, which differs from the earlier function, and what its certificate covers.

What is not supported

  • Inputs other than a dense Matrix{Float32} or Matrix{Float64} with a Vector of the same element type: integer, sparse and view designs, matrix responses and mixed element types are refused with an ArgumentError; nothing is converted.
  • An intercept, standardization and observation weights.
  • Penalty paths, screening, continuation and cross-validation.
  • Weighted, adaptive, feasible and scaled lasso; nonconvex penalties; generalized linear models.
  • Group, sparse-group and multitask regression; quantile regression; SLOPE and knockoffs; structured and fused penalties; low-rank and sparse-plus-low-rank models; varying-coefficient models; post-selection inference.
  • General coordinate-descent interfaces, the compatibility API of earlier versions, and extensions for HiGHS, Knockoffs, ProximalAlgorithms and StatsModels.

The complete earlier package, with its documentation, examples and changelog, is preserved on the branch legacy/full and the tag archive/pre-minimal-core/master.

HighDimensionalModels

The Gaussian lasso at one penalty level, lasso, with an optional intercept and column scales, as a thin layer on ProximalBase (the loss and the penalty) and ProximalSolvers (coordinate descent).

The square-root lasso at one penalty level, sqrt_lasso, minimizes the normalized ‖y - β₀1 - Xβ‖/√n + λ‖θ‖₁ with ProximalSolvers' square-root coordinate descent and its duality certificate.
